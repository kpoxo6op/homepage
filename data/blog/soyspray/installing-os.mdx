## Autoinstall

I picked Ubuntu Server due to its popularity (easy troubleshooting online) and
human friendly YAML file for automatic installation.

It took me some time to understand Canonical customizations on top of Cloud
Init. I have found these notes
https://medium.com/@Phoenixforge/diving-deeper-with-ubuntu-autoinstall-b15e5bdffbb5
invaluable for understanding the Ubuntu autoinstall flavor.
