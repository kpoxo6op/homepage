## Theo’s tip

Theo from t3.gg pinned a post saying agents can be good at UI “if you burn enough tokens”. His method: one model builds, other models judge screenshots, the builder fixes what they find, and the loop repeats.

- He puts his own UI experiment at about \$400 and 17.6 hours of agent time.
- He says a \$200 subscription “can do \$12,000 of usage in a month”.
- His cost dashboard shows about \$75.6K in total, two-thirds of it on Claude Opus. TODO: the post with this screenshot, and the period it covers.

## Round three: the edge fix

On a Pixel, the new slider could get so close to the screen edge that Android’s back gesture took over. Earlier versions of the site avoided this on purpose. A short third round moved the line back from both edges.

- Status: the fix and its review are done, but it is not deployed yet. TODO: add a video once the preview is live.
