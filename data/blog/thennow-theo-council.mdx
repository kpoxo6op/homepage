## Theo’s post

Theo (t3.gg) pinned a post saying agents can be good at UI “if you burn enough tokens”, with a video explaining the method. The method is a screenshot review loop: one model builds, independent models score real screenshots, the builder fixes what they found, and the loop repeats.

### How much Theo spends

- In the video, Theo puts the UI experiment at about 17.6 hours of parallel agent time and “roughly 400 or so dollars” at list prices, with \$152 for one of the runs.
- He says it came out of his subscriptions rather than paid API usage, and that a \$200 subscription “can do \$12,000 of usage in a month”.
- A screenshot of Theo’s cost dashboard shows about \$75.6K in total: Standard \$64,104.48, Fast \$445.88, Ultrafast \$11,074.59, with Premium \$9,380.98 shown separately.
- By model: claude-opus-5-5 \$49,576.41 (65.6%, 147B tokens), muse-proxy-wonder-full \$11,093.21 (14.7%, 14.1B), gpt-6-astra \$8,880.22 (11.7%, 1.10B), gpt-6.1-sol \$3,950.24 (5.2%, 6.15B), gemini-4-argon \$821.33 (1.1%, 2.71B).

## What I told GPT to do

Everything ran from one Codex (GPT) thread. Codex did not design anything itself. It set up [T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code), wrote the briefs, started the runs, checked the results and sent the previews to Telegram through the Soyclaw bot.

Instruction to Codex, 18:24:

> Forget Dennison, probably it is not for me. Next experiment for you is to apply Theo’s approach. I have Claude and GPT subs. Claude has more usage so lean on it more. Watch what Theo explains and do a few rounds of his approach on a new home screen mock, make it target to become much better than what it is now. Use Theo’s T3 to set it up and run as he describes. Share results with me on Telegram in Soyclaw. Don’t delete old previews.

_Lightly cleaned up from the original typed message._

Setup used in all runs:

- **Builder:** Claude Opus 5.5 in T3 Code.
- **Reviewers:** a fresh Claude Opus 5.5 and a fresh GPT-6.1 Sol for every round, both on high effort, started with T3’s `delegate_task`.
- **Skill:** Theo’s original [council skill](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack/blob/1085050f3baed1ac724caa4a8166145fb2ac88af/skills/council/SKILL.md) from `t3dotgg/tstack`.
- **Subscriptions only:** Claude and ChatGPT plans, the two-provider setup Theo mentions in the video (22:20–22:56). No API keys or paid credits.
- **Blind scoring:** reviewers never saw earlier scores, the round number, the target or each other’s opinions.
- **Fixed references:** every round compared the candidate with the same frozen screenshots of the two old V2 previews.
- **Guard rails:** all 306 entries had to stay byte-identical, old previews stayed up, and lint, typecheck, build and the browser test suite had to pass.

## The edge-drag fix

On a Pixel, the new comparison line could go so close to the screen edge that Android’s back gesture took over. Earlier versions of the site kept the line away from the edges on purpose. The request at 21:58 was “Do another short t3 on this, same rules”.

- A short third run on the same branch: one fix and one focused council review.
- Local commits `dc82fd0` and `be0f86f` (22:04–22:06, not pushed yet) keep the line and its handle clear of both screen edges on touch.
- **Status when this draft was written:** still running; T3 had paused on a cleanup command that Codex declined.
